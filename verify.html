NOVA · proof receipt check

Check a receipt

Paste a whole proof receipt, or just its 64-character fingerprint. NOVA tells you whether this NOVA issued it and whether it has been changed. Nothing is stored and nothing from your paste is shown back.

A receipt shows what NOVA saw. A match means the text is exactly what this NOVA issued; it does not mean anyone read, accepted or did anything. A "self-reported" receipt is only as strong as the person who reported it.
